# Asbestos Shed Removal: What Fibro Sheds and Garages Cost to Take Down

The old fibro shed down the back has probably been there longer than you have. It's sagging a bit, the sheeting's gone chalky, and you're thinking about clearing the yard. Before anyone swings a crowbar, you want to know what that shed is likely made of, what taking it down properly involves, and what the job typically costs. The prices below are Melbourne-metro benchmarks, because no one publishes Geelong-specific pricing. For the wider picture, see our [Geelong asbestos removal cost guide](/blog/asbestos-removal-cost-geelong/).

## Why fibro sheds and garages so often contain asbestos

Asbestos cement was widely used in Australian building from the mid-1940s until the late 1980s, and sheds and garages were an obvious use for it: cheap flat sheeting, quick to nail up, tough enough for a backyard. Plenty of Geelong's older suburbs were built in exactly that window, so a fibro garage behind a post-war house is a reasonable thing to be suspicious of.

Suspicious isn't the same as confirmed. A sheet that looks like asbestos cement might be, and a sheet that looks modern might not be. Only a lab test can confirm it. Our guide to [what asbestos looks like](/blog/what-does-asbestos-look-like/) helps you decide what to sample, and the [Geelong testing page](/asbestos-testing-geelong/) covers how. A NATA-accredited laboratory analyses the sample. Each different material needs its own sample, so a shed with wall sheeting, a roof and an eaves strip can mean three samples.

## What the job involves

A licensed removalist doesn't just knock the shed over. For a non-friable fibro shed, a Class B licence holder will typically:

- notify WorkSafe Victoria before starting (24 hours for 10 m2 or less of non-friable material, five days for anything bigger)
- set up signage and control the area
- wet the sheeting down so fibres stay put
- take the sheets off whole, not broken
- wrap them in polythene sheeting about 200 microns thick, seal it with tape and label it with the asbestos warning mark
- carry the waste to a landfill authorised to receive asbestos
- clean up and decontaminate the site

If the shed or garage is larger than 10 m2 of asbestos cement, which is true of most of them, a clearance certificate from an independent person is also required before the area is reoccupied. At your own home, the duty to obtain it sits with the licence holder, not with you.

## Why most sheds are past the 10 m2 and one-hour caps

Victoria lets someone remove a small amount of non-friable asbestos without a licence only if two caps both hold: no more than 10 m2 in total, and no more than one hour of removal work in any seven-day period. Both caps have to be met.

Now measure your shed. A small 4 by 3 metre shed has 14 metres of wall around it. At 2 metres high, that's 28 m2 of wall sheeting before you count the roof, a gable or a lean-to. A garage is bigger again. And an hour is not much time to carefully wet, unscrew and wrap dozens of sheets.

A point on accuracy: those caps are workplace rules. They bind employers and self-employed people doing the work as a job. WorkSafe's compliance code says a person removing asbestos at their own home isn't covered, because a home isn't a workplace, though it recommends homeowners follow the same code. The moment a tradie, a tenant, a rental or a paid helper is involved, the caps are law. Our [guide to removing asbestos yourself in Victoria](/blog/can-i-remove-asbestos-myself-victoria/) goes through it. The real reason sheds usually go to a licensed removalist is simply that they're big jobs with a lot of material to handle safely.

## What a shed or garage removal typically costs

These are Melbourne-metro benchmarks, drawn from published Victorian figures. Treat them as a starting point for a conversation, not a quote.

| Job | Typical Melbourne-metro range (AUD) |
|---|---|
| Fibro shed | $1,300 to $3,000 |
| Single garage | $2,500 to $5,000 |
| Double garage | $4,000 to $8,000 |
| Concrete slab removal (separate line) | $60 to $180 per square metre |

Prices are indicative and GST treatment varies between providers, so ask each removalist whether their quote includes GST.

What moves a price within each range: how much sheeting there is, whether the roof is also asbestos cement, how easy it is to get at the shed, and how far the removalist has to travel. Any asbestos job also carries a minimum charge, which Melbourne-metro benchmarks put at $800 to $1,500, so a tiny lean-to isn't cheap just because it's tiny. Each quote should show the removalist's WorkSafe Victoria licence number, so you can compare like with like.

## Super Six roofs on sheds

Some sheds and garages carry a corrugated asbestos cement roof, commonly called Super Six, rather than flat sheeting alone. It's non-friable when intact, and it's removed under the same rules, but it adds area and it adds height, which affects how the removalist sets up the job. If your shed roof is corrugated and old, see our [Super Six asbestos roof removal guide](/blog/super-six-asbestos-roof-removal/) for how those sheets are handled. When you're collecting quotes, make sure each one says whether the roof is included.

## What stays: the slab

Taking the shed down doesn't mean the concrete goes with it. The slab usually stays. If the slab itself needs to come up, because it's cracked or contaminated or you want the space, that's priced separately by the removalist at $60 to $180 per square metre. Ask for it as its own line so you can decide whether you want it.

Asbestos in soil and fill under or around old sheds is a separate issue. See [asbestos in soil and contaminated fill](/blog/asbestos-in-soil-contaminated-fill/) if you find broken fragments in the ground.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does asbestos shed removal cost in Victoria?

Melbourne-metro benchmarks put a fibro shed at $1,300 to $3,000, a single garage at $2,500 to $5,000 and a double garage at $4,000 to $8,000. No Geelong-specific pricing is published, so use these as a starting point and get written quotes. Prices are indicative and GST treatment varies between providers, so ask each removalist whether their quote includes GST.

### Can I knock down my own fibro shed?

Victoria's 10 square metre and one-hour caps are workplace rules, and WorkSafe's compliance code says they don't apply to a person at their own home, though it recommends following the code anyway. Most sheds hold well over 10 square metres of sheeting and take far longer than an hour, which is why they usually go to a licensed removalist. Test first, and talk to your GP if you're worried about past exposure.

### Does the concrete slab get removed with the shed?

Usually not. The slab typically stays unless you ask for it to come up, and if you do, removalists price it separately at roughly $60 to $180 per square metre. Ask for it as a distinct line on the quote.

### Who puts up the new shed afterwards?

You do, with a registered builder or shed supplier of your choice. Asbestos removal and building work are separate jobs, and we don't quote or carry out the replacement.
