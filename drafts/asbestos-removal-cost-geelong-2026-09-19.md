# Asbestos Removal Cost in Geelong: What the Quotes Actually Look Like

Four cracked sheets at the bottom of a fibro fence sound like a small, cheap job. They are not. Victorian removalists publish a minimum job charge of $800 to $1,500, and that one figure explains more surprised phone calls than anything else in this trade.

Asbestos removal cost in Geelong is hard to pin down, for an honest reason. Nobody in Geelong or the wider Barwon region publishes a price list. Every figure below comes from Victorian suppliers, Victorian councils or national cost guides, and all of them are Melbourne metro benchmarks rather than Geelong rates. Two of those suppliers price regional work separately anyway.

Prices here are indicative and GST treatment varies between providers, so ask each removalist whether their quote includes GST. None of these numbers is a quote from us.

## Why the minimum job charge sets the floor on asbestos removal cost

Fibro fencing runs roughly $20 to $50 a sheet to remove and dispose of. That figure is real, and on its own it is misleading, because very little residential work lands under the $800 to $1,500 minimum.

The minimum covers what happens whether the job is four sheets or forty. A licensed removalist has to notify WorkSafe before starting, control the work area, take the material down whole rather than breaking it, then wrap and label every package to EPA requirements. After that someone drives a legal load to a tip authorised to receive it.

None of that gets cheaper because your pile is small. Which is why a per sheet rate tells you almost nothing about what a small patch will actually cost.

If you are weighing up a repair against doing the whole run, this is the number that decides it. Two sheets and twenty sheets are much closer in price than most people expect.

## Asbestos testing cost comes first, and it is the cheap part

You cannot identify asbestos by eye, and no removalist should price a job on a guess. A single sample analysed by a NATA accredited laboratory runs $88 to $160.

Most houses need three to eight samples, because every different material has to be tested separately. A fence, an eaves lining and a bathroom wall sheet are three samples, not one. Larger properties run to eight or twelve.

Labs price this two different ways. Some charge a flat rate per sample however many you send, and some drop the price from the second sample onwards. Worth asking which one you are dealing with before you post off a box of five.

If you would rather someone else took the samples, an on-site inspection with sampling included runs $395 to $900 on a Melbourne metro basis. Standard lab turnaround is one to five business days. Expect a surcharge of 25% to 50% for a result inside 24 to 48 hours.

Testing is also the cleanest first step legally, and the cheapest way to stop worrying. Our [asbestos testing page](/asbestos-testing-geelong/) covers what a lab report actually tells you and what to do with a positive result.

## Where the 10 square metre rule really sits

The limit everyone quotes is 10 square metres. The rule has a second half that keeps getting dropped: non-friable material may be removed without a licence only where the area does not exceed 10 square metres in total, and no more than one hour of asbestos removal work is done in any seven day period. Both caps have to hold, and the hour is cumulative across jobs rather than per job.

Then there is the part most asbestos content gets wrong. Those regulations bind employers and self-employed people at workplaces, and [WorkSafe Victoria's compliance code](https://www.worksafe.vic.gov.au/resources/compliance-code-removing-asbestos-workplaces) states that a home is not treated as a workplace when the owner is doing the work there.

So it is not accurate to say a homeowner breaks the law by removing more than 10 square metres at their own house. The accurate version is duller. That is the limit the law sets for anyone doing this as work, WorkSafe recommends homeowners follow the same code anyway, and the caps become law the moment a tradesperson, a tenant, a rental property or a paid helper is involved. Friable material is never covered at any quantity.

Victoria also has no "licensed asbestos assessor" class, whatever a NSW page tells you. The licences here are Class A, covering friable asbestos and everything below it, and Class B, covering non-friable only. Almost all residential work around Geelong is Class B. The DIY question is worked through properly in [can I remove asbestos myself in Victoria](/blog/can-i-remove-asbestos-myself-victoria/).

There is a practical consequence for your timeline. A licence holder must notify WorkSafe at least 24 hours before a job of 10 square metres or less of non-friable material, and at least five days before anything larger. That duty sits on the removalist, never on you. It is also why nobody credible can start tomorrow morning on a big job.

## What is not in the quote

Replacement. A removal quote covers taking the material away and nothing else. A new fence, a re-roof, re-cladding or making good is separate work you arrange directly with a registered builder, and a quote that bundles removal and replacement into one number is quoting domestic building work.

A concrete slab under a garage is priced separately, roughly $60 to $180 a square metre, and plenty of people leave theirs in place.

The clearance certificate is usually its own line. Above 10 square metres of Class A or Class B work it is required, and it has to come from an independent person who is not the removalist and has no financial interest in the job. On a domestic job that duty sits with the removalist rather than with you, which is worth knowing before you agree to chase one yourself. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does asbestos removal cost in Geelong?

There is no published Geelong price list, so the best anyone can give you is a Victorian range. A whole fibro fence runs $1,000 to $3,500, a house roof $4,000 to $15,000, and general removal $45 to $150 a square metre. Below those, the $800 to $1,500 minimum job charge is what most small quotes come back at.

### Is it cheaper to remove just the damaged sheets?

Usually less than you would hope. Sheets cost $20 to $50 each to remove and dispose of, but the minimum job charge of $800 to $1,500 applies either way, so a four sheet repair and a twelve sheet repair often land close together. Get the whole run quoted alongside the patch before you decide.

### Do I need a clearance certificate, and who pays for it?

A clearance certificate is required for Class A or Class B removal above 10 square metres in total. It must be issued by an independent person who is not the removalist, and it typically costs $165 to $250 for a visual clearance on a residential job. Where the work is at a home and the owner engaged the removalist, the duty to obtain it sits on the licence holder.

### Can I take asbestos to my local tip?

Only if that tip is licensed to receive it, and in the Barwon region only Drysdale Landfill is. Surf Coast Shire refuses asbestos at every transfer station, and Colac Otway publishes no option. Call Drysdale on (03) 5251 2935 first, as they take small domestic quantities only and may need you to book a time.
